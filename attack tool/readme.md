\# /attack – Legal OSINT Multi‑Tool



/attack è un multi‑tool OSINT \*\*legale\*\* in Python, con animazioni, colori personalizzabili e supporto a:



\- Termux

\- Kali Linux

\- Ubuntu/Debian

\- Arch

\- Windows (WSL)



\## Funzioni principali



\- Social OSINT: Instagram, TikTok, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp (solo scraping e web)

\- Email OSINT: holehe, epieos, emailrep, hunter (web)

\- Phone OSINT: phoneinfoga

\- Username OSINT: Sherlock, Maigret

\- IP OSINT: ipinfo.io

\- Domain OSINT: whois

\- Geo OSINT: Google Maps

\- Device info: neofetch

\- OSINT Web Hub: osintframework.com
